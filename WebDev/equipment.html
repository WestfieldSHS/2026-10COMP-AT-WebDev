Evidence Finders

EMF Reader: Detects electronic variations. Gives a Level 5 reading for specific entities. Can be ticked off if flickering lights are present

Spirit Box: Radio device used to talk to the entity. Requires local voice proximity. It should show a prompt if enabled in settings.

Thermometer: Measures room temperature. Used to find Freezing Temperatures. Visuals could be seeing your own or others breath or frozen windows.

Laser Projector: Emits grids of light. Reveals silhouettes of moving spirits.

Spirit Book: Blank book left on the ground. Entities will interact and leave Inscriptions.

Blacklight: Emits UV light. Reveals hidden glowing prints on doors and switches. Prints can be easier to find using the "Salt Canister".

Video Camera: Placed on tripods. Used to safely watch for Ghost Orbs from the van. Watch out for the "Skinwalker" Ghost..

Plushie & Flower Pot: Passive sanity items. Dropping them in rooms helps slow down ambient sanity drain. They can both be used as evidence. (Spirit box and Wither)

Cross (Crucifix): Burnable protection tool. Prevents a hunt if the entity attacks near it. To identify the "Demon" the cross may float and spin in the air.

Holy Oil: Defensive ward. Can be poured to temporarily deter aggressive manifestations. Helps with identifying "The Wisp" and the "Spirit" ghosts.

Salt Canister: Deployable floor hazard. Entities stepping in it reveal movement paths. Helps identifying the "Wraith" and "Specter" ghosts.

Photo Camera: Takes photos for an increase of income. Can allow to identify the "Dullahan"

Other

Lighter: Ignition utility. Used to light candles around the map to preserve ambient sanity.
LIDAR Scanner: Maps physical rooms using 3D dots. Reveals shapes or blockages where entities stand.
Flashlight & Lantern: Main portable light sources. Vital for exploring dark rooms.
Rock Salt Shotgun: Late-game self-defense tool. Temporarily blinds or repels hunting entities.
Defibrillator: Emergency medical item. Revives a fallen teammate back into the round.
Energy Drink: Consumable stamina boost. Temporarily grants infinite sprint to escape hunts.
Energy Watch: UI utility tracker. Keeps real-time tracking of your stamina levels.
