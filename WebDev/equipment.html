EMF Reader: Detects electronic variations. Gives a Level 5 reading for specific entities.
Spirit Box: Radio device used to talk to the entity. Requires local voice proximity.
Thermometer: Measures room temperature. Used to find Freezing Temperatures.
Laser Projector: Emits grids of light. Reveals silhouettes of moving spirits.
Spirit Book: Blank book left on the ground. Entities will interact and leave Inscriptions.
Blacklight: Emits UV light. Reveals hidden glowing prints on doors and switches.
LIDAR Scanner: Maps physical rooms using 3D dots. Reveals shapes or blockages where entities stand.
Video Camera: Placed on tripods. Used to safely watch for Ghost Orbs from the van.
Flashlight & Lantern: Main portable light sources. Vital for exploring dark rooms.
Cross (Crucifix): Burnable protection tool. Prevents a hunt if the entity attacks near it.
Holy Oil: Defensive ward. Can be poured to temporarily deter aggressive manifestations.
Salt Canister: Deployable floor hazard. Entities stepping in it reveal movement paths.
Rock Salt Shotgun: Late-game self-defense tool. Temporarily blinds or repels hunting entities.
Defibrillator: Emergency medical item. Revives a fallen teammate back into the round.
Energy Drink: Consumable stamina boost. Temporarily grants infinite sprint to escape hunts.
Energy Watch: UI utility tracker. Keeps real-time tracking of your stamina levels.
Plushie & Flower Pot: Passive sanity items. Dropping them in rooms helps slow down ambient sanity drain.
Lighter: Ignition utility. Used to light candles around the map to preserve ambient sanity.
