Blacklight

Emits UV light. Reveals hidden glowing prints on doors and switches. Prints can be easier to find using the "Salt Canister".

Video Camera

Plushie & Flower Pot

Passive sanity items. Dropping them in rooms helps slow down ambient sanity drain. They can both be used as evidence. (Spirit box and Wither)

Crucifix

Burnable protection tool. Prevents a hunt if the entity attacks near it. To identify the "Demon" the cross may float and spin in the air.

Holy Oil

Defensive ward. Can be poured to temporarily deter aggressive manifestations. Helps with identifying "The Wisp" and the "Spirit" ghosts.

Salt Canister

Deployable floor hazard. Entities stepping in it reveal movement paths. Helps identifying the "Wraith" and "Specter" ghosts.
